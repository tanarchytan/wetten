# Protocol bij het Noord-Atlantisch Verdrag betreffende de toetreding van Roemenië

> **BWB-id:** BWBV0001632 · **Type:** verdrag · **Ministerie:** Buitenlandse Zaken
> **Geldend:** 2004-02-27 – heden
> [Overzicht](./README.md)

### Artikel I — Article I

Article I North Atlantic Treaty article 10 of the Treaty Upon the entry into force of this Protocol, the Secretary General of the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation shall, on behalf of all the Parties, communicate to the Government of Romania an invitation to accede to the. In accordance with, Romania shall become a Party on the date when it deposits its instrument of accession with the Government of the United States of America.

### Artikel II — Article II

Article II North Atlantic Treaty The present Protocol shall enter into force when each of the Parties to thehas notified the Government of the United States of America of its acceptance thereof. The Government of the United States of America shall inform all the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty of the date of receipt of each such notification and of the date of the entry into force of the present Protocol.

### Artikel III — Article III

Article III North Atlantic Treaty The present Protocol of which the English and French texts are equally authentic, shall be deposited in the Archives of the Government of the United States of America. Duly certified copies thereof shall be transmitted by that Government to the Governments of all the Parties to the.
