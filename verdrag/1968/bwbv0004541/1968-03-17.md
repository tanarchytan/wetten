### Paragraaf Afdeling III
### Artikel 1

1 This Protocol shall be open to signature by the member States. It shall be subject to ratification. Instruments of ratification shall be deposited with the Secretary-General, provided that the Committee of Ministers in appropriate cases has previously given an affirmative decision as provided for in Section IV, paragraph 4. 1965 47 24-03-1965 1967 53 27-04-

### Artikel 2

2 This Protocol shall enter into force one year after the date of the deposit of the third instrument of ratification. 1965 47 24-03-1965 1967 53 27-04-

### Artikel 3

3 As regards any Signatory ratifying subsequently, this Protocol shall enter into force one year after the date of deposit of its instrument of ratification. 1965 47 24-03-1965 1967 53 27-04-
