# Protocol tot bestrijding van wederrechtelijke daden van geweld op luchthavens voor de internationale burgerluchtvaart bij het Verdrag tot bestrijding van wederrechtelijke gedragingen tegen de veiligheid van de burgerluchtvaart, gedaan te Montreal op 23 september 1971

### Artikel I — Article I

Article I Convention for the Suppression of Unlawful Acts against the Safety of Civil Aviation This Protocol supplements the, done at Montreal on 23 September 1971 (hereinafter referred to as “the Convention”), and, as between the Parties to this Protocol, the Convention and the Protocol shall be read and interpreted together as one single instrument.

### Artikel II — Article II

Article II 1 bis: In Article 1 of the Convention, the following shall be added as new paragraph 1 "1 bis. Any person commits an offence if he unlawfully and intentionally, using any device, substance or weapon: (a) performs an act of violence against a person at an airport serving international civil aviation which causes or is likely to cause serious injury or death; or (b) destroys or seriously damages the facilities of an airport serving international civil aviation or aircraft not in service located thereon or disrupts the services of the airport, if such an act endangers or is likely to endanger safety at that airport." 2 In paragraph 2 (a) of Article 1 of the Convention, the following words shall be inserted after the words "paragraph 1": bis". ### Artikel III — Article III

Article III bis: In Article 5 of the Convention, the following shall be added as paragraph 2 "2 bis. bis, Each Contracting State shall likewise take such measures as may be necessary to establish its jurisdiction over the offences mentioned in Article 1, paragraph 1and in Article 1, paragraph 2, in so far as that paragraph relates to those offences, in the case where the alleged offender is present in its territory and it does not extradite him pursuant to Article 8 to the State mentioned in paragraph 1 (a) of this Article."

### Artikel IV — Article IV

Article IV This Protocol shall be open for signature at Montreal on 24 February 1988 by States participating in the International Conference on Air Law held at Montreal from 9 to 24 February 1988. After 1 March 1988, the Protocol shall be open for signature to all States in London Moscow, Washington and Montreal, until it enters into force in accordance with Article VI.

### Artikel V — Article V

Article V 1 This Protocol shall be subject to ratification by the signatory States. 2 Convention Article 15 Any State which is not a Contracting State to themay ratify this Protocol if at the same time it ratifies or accedes to the Convention in accordance withthereof. 3 Instruments of ratification shall be deposited with the Governments of the Union of Soviet Socialist Republics, the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland and the United States of America or with the International Civil Aviation Organization, which are hereby designated the Depositaries.

### Artikel VI — Article VI

Article VI 1 As soon as ten of the signatory States have deposited their instruments of ratification of this Protocol, it shall enter into force between them on the thirtieth day after the date of the deposit of the tenth instrument of ratification. Artikel VII 1 Het Protocol staat, nadat het in werking is getreden, open voor toetreding door niet-ondertekenende Staten. 2 Verdrag artikel 15 van het Verdrag Elke Staat die geen Partij is bij hetkan tot dit Protocol toetreden, indien hij tegelijkertijd het Verdrag bekrachtigt of daartoe toetreedt in overeenstemming met. 3 Akten van toetreding worden nedergelegd bij de depositarissen en de toetreding wordt van kracht op de dertigste dag na de nederlegging.
