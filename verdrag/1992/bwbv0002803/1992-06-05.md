# Slotdocument van de Buitengewone Conferentie van Staten Partij bij het Verdrag inzake conventionele strijdkrachten in Europa

> **BWB-id:** BWBV0002803 · **Type:** verdrag · **Ministerie:** Buitenlandse Zaken
> **Geldend:** 1992-06-05 – heden
> [Overzicht](./README.md)

### Artikel 1

1 Treaty The understandings, notifications, confirmations and commitments contained or referred to in this Final Document and its Annexes A en B, together with the deposit of instruments of ratification by all the States Parties, shall be deemed as fulfilling the requirements for the entry into force of thein accordance with its provisions. Accordingly, the Treaty shall enter into force 10 days after the last such instrument has been deposited.

### Artikel 2

2 Treaty on Conventional Armed Forces in Europe In this context, the States Parties note the Agreement of May 15, 1992, on the Principles and Procedures of Implementation of the, the four Protocols to that Agreement and the Joint Declaration of May 15, 1992, in relation to the Treaty on Conventional Armed Forces in Europe, as transmitted on June 1, 1992, by that Agreement's Depositary to all States Parties to the Treaty. In this regard, Articles 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 10, 11 and 12 of that Agreement, the four Protocols to that Agreement, and the Joint Declaration of May 15, 1992, in relation to the Treaty on Conventional Armed Forces in Europe contain necessary confirmations and information.

### Artikel 3

3 The States Parties confirm the understandings as elaborated in the Joint Consultative Group, and specified in Annex A of this Final Document.

### Artikel 4

4 The States Parties confirm all decisions and recommendations adopted by the Joint Consultative Group.

### Artikel 5

5 Treaty This Final Document in no way alters the rights and obligations of the States Parties as set forth in theand its associated documents.

### Artikel 6

6 This Final Document shall enter into force upon signature by all of the States Parties.

### Artikel 7

7 Treaty This Final Document, together with its Annexes A en B, which are integral to it, in all the official languages of the Conference on Security and Cooperation in Europe, shall be deposited with the Government of the Kingdom of the Netherlands, as the designated Depositary for the, which shall circulate copies of this Final Document to all the States Parties.
