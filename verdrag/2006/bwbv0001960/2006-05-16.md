# Notawisseling houdende een verdrag tussen het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden en het Koninkrijk België betreffende de status van militair en burgerpersoneel van de Belgische strijdkrachten in de Nederlandse Antillen en Aruba voor de oefening "Caribbean Lion 2006"

> **BWB-id:** BWBV0001960 · **Type:** verdrag · **Ministerie:** Buitenlandse Zaken
> **Geldend:** 2006-05-16 – 2007-05-15
> [Overzicht](./README.md) · [Volgende versie (2007-05-16) →](./2007-05-16.md)

### Artikel I — Article I Temporary Stationing of Belgian Armed Forces

Article I Temporary Stationing of Belgian Armed Forces For the purpose of this Agreement, military and civilian personnel of the Belgian Armed Forces, their vessels and military equipment may be stationed temporarily in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba for the exercise ‘‘Caribbean Lion 2006’’.

### Artikel II — Article II Use of Facilities

Article II Use of Facilities Military and civilian personnel of the Belgian Armed Forces may use military facilities provided by the Flag Officer Royal Netherlands Forces in the Caribbean under conditions to be agreed upon between the Flag Officer Royal Netherlands Forces in the Caribbean and the Rear Admiral (l.h.), Commander of the Maritime Component to the Belgian Navy.

### Artikel III — Article III Applicability of NATO SOFA

Article III Applicability of NATO SOFA Except as otherwise provided for in this Agreement, Articles I through XIII of the Agreement between the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty regarding the Status of their Forces, done at London on 19 June 1951 (hereafter, the NATO SOFA) shall apply, mutatis mutandis. To this effect, for purposes of this Agreement, references and provisions in the NATO SOFA to the North Atlantic Treaty, the North Atlantic Council, the North Atlantic Treaty Organization and to Article XX therein shall be inapplicable.

### Artikel IV — Article IV Settlement of Disputes

Article IV Settlement of Disputes Any disputes or disagreements that may arise from the application or implementation of this Agreement shall be settled through consultation between the appropriate authorities of the Parties, including as necessary, through diplomatic channels.

### Artikel V — Article V Territorial Applicability

Article V Territorial Applicability This Agreement shall apply to the territory of the Kingdom of the Netherlands in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba.

### Artikel VI — Article VI Duration

Article VI Duration This Agreement shall remain in force for the duration of the presence of the military and civilian personnel of the Belgian Armed Forces in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba for the exercise ‘‘Caribbean Lion 2006’’ but for no longer than one year from the date this Agreement enters into force. Notwithstanding, the provisions of this Agreement regarding the adjudication and payment of claims shall remain in force for such time as is necessary to resolve any outstanding claims arising under this Agreement.
