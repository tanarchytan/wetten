# Notawisseling houdende een verdrag tussen het Koninkrijk der Nederlanden en het Verenigd Koninkrijk van Groot Brittannië en Noord-Ierland betreffende de status van militair en burgerpersoneel van de Britse strijdkrachten in de Nederlandse Antillen en Aruba voor de oefening "Caribbean Lion 2006"

> **BWB-id:** BWBV0001959 · **Type:** verdrag · **Ministerie:** Buitenlandse Zaken
> **Geldend:** 2006-05-12 – 2007-05-11
> [Overzicht](./README.md) · [Volgende versie (2007-05-12) →](./2007-05-12.md)

### Artikel I — Article I Temporary Stationing of UK Armed Forces

Article I Temporary Stationing of UK Armed Forces For the purpose of this Agreement, military and civilian personnel of the UK Armed Forces, their vessels and military equipment may be stationed temporarily in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba for the exercise ‘‘Caribbean Lion 2006’’.

### Artikel II — Article II Use of Facilities

Article II Use of Facilities Military and civilian personnel of the UK Armed Forces may use military facilities provided by the Flag Officer Royal Netherlands Forces in the Caribbean under conditions to be agreed upon between the Flag Officer Royal Netherlands Forces in the Caribbean and the Royal Naval Chief of Staff-Capability of the United Kingdom of Great Britain and Northern Ireland.

### Artikel III — Article III Security of Personnel and Information

Article III Security of Personnel and Information All classified information and material exchanged or generated in connection with this Agreement will be used, handled and safeguarded in accordance with the General Security Arrangement between the Parties dated 29 September 1994.

### Artikel IV — Article IV Applicability of NATO SOFA

Article IV Applicability of NATO SOFA Except as otherwise provided for in this Arrangement, Articles I through XIII of the Arrangement between the Parties to the North Atlantic Treaty regarding the Status of their Forces, done at London on 19 June 1951 (hereafter, the NATO SOFA) will apply, mutatis mutandis.

### Artikel V — Article V Settlement of Disputes

Article V Settlement of Disputes Any disputes or disagreements that may arise from the application or implementation of this Agreement shall be settled through consultation between the appropriate authorities of the Parties, including, as necessary, through diplomatic channels.

### Artikel VI — Article VI Territorial Applicability

Article VI Territorial Applicability With regard to the Kingdom of the Netherlands, this Agreement shall apply to its territory in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba.

### Artikel VII — Article VII Interpretation

Article VII Interpretation For purposes of this Agreement, references and provisions in the NATO SOFA to the North Atlantic Treaty, the North Atlantic Council and the North Atlantic Treaty Organisation shall be inapplicable.

### Artikel VIII — Article VIII Duration

Article VIII Duration This Agreement shall remain in effect for the duration of the presence of the military and civilian personnel of the UK Armed Forces in the Netherlands Antilles and Aruba for the exercise ‘‘Caribbean Lion 2006’’ but for no longer than one year from the date this arrangement enters into force. Notwithstanding, the provisions of this Agreement regarding the adjudication and payment of claims shall remain in force for such time as is necessary to resolve any outstanding claims arising under the Agreement.
